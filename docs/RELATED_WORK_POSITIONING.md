# Nearest-work positioning — source-checked 2026-11-07

This is a focused nearest-neighbor comparison, not an exhaustive novelty search. No first-ever claim is established.

| Primary source | Existing evidence | Increment and limit of this project |
|---|---|---|
| [Robustness Analysis, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.22676v2), §§II-B–II-C | Qwen3-8B scores paired retail tool-return conditions. It compares content likelihood under schema/full prefix and entropy over legal next actions, not mean vocabulary-token entropy. | Our study varies visible table-row evidence and audits scalar numerical checks. Different entropy definitions prevent interpreting our low mean-token-entropy performance as a refutation of its action-distribution signal. |
| [ToxicBench, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37153v1), §3 and Appendix E.2 | Already studies corrupted-return adoption, checking and recovery. E.2 uses 24 public-table tasks, fixed-parent structured evidence, partial/full aliases and controlled subsequent evidence. | Our potential contribution is a scoped audit of visible-row budgets, actual exposure, prompted judgments versus a structured statistical program, and repeated row draws. Public-table poisoning or evidence manipulation alone is not new. |

The closest prior was incorrectly described in our state file as a closed-model study; §II-C explicitly reports Qwen3-8B. The corrected state entry and this comparison replace that description. The two projects also have different domains, score inputs and research units; neither comparison establishes a new method advantage here.

The defensible contribution is a reproducible empirical case: distinguish displayed evidence, statistical extraction and observed model behavior under numerical multiplicative corruption. It is not a universal impossibility result, a new bootstrap algorithm, a discovered internal mechanism, or a deployed safety policy. Fresh-table raw-versus-derived-summary controls could test an additional mechanism-oriented hypothesis, but those results do not yet exist.
